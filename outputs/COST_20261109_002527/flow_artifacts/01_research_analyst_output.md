### Business Model and Operations
Costco operates on a membership warehouse model, offering low prices on a limited selection of nationally-branded and private-label products in a wide range of categories. They achieve operating efficiencies through volume purchasing, efficient distribution, and reduced handling of merchandise in no-frills, self-service warehouse facilities (10-K Line 801). Costco buys most of its merchandise directly from suppliers and routes it to cross-docking consolidation points or directly to their warehouses (10-K Line 802).

### Competitive Position and Moat
Costco's competitive position is influenced by the health of the economies in which they do business, including the effects of inflation or deflation (10-K Line 1308). They believe that their pricing authority, consistently providing the most competitive values to members, is a key factor in their success (10-K Line 1309). However, they also face significant competition from other retailers and wholesalers.

### Catalysts and Recent Developments
Recent developments include the impact of IEEPA tariffs on Costco's operations. In February 2025, the United States imposed tariffs under the International Emergency Economic Powers Act (IEEPA) on certain goods, materials, and products imported into the United States (10-K Line 1346). However, in February 2026, the U.S. Supreme Court ruled that the IEEPA tariffs were unlawful, leading to a program to administer IEEPA refunds (10-K Line 1347).

### Insight:
Costco operates 939 warehouses worldwide as of August 30, 2026 (EX-99.1 Line 45).

Insight: Paid members, in most countries, are eligible to upgrade to an Executive membership for an additional annual fee. These members earn an Executive reward, generally 2% on qualified purchases, subject to an annual maximum value, redeemable at Costco warehouses. Executive members represented 42,300, 38,700, and 35,400 of total paid members in 2026, 2025, and 2024. The penetration of sales to Executive members represented approximately 75.2% of worldwide net sales in 2026. [10-K Line 858]
