## 1. Overview

### Business Model and Operations
Uber's business model is based on a technology platform that connects consumers with providers of ride services, merchants, and delivery service providers for meal preparation, grocery, and other delivery services. The company operates in three segments: Mobility, Delivery, and Freight. Uber's platform uses a massive network, leading technology, operational excellence, and product expertise to power movement from point A to point B.

[10-K Line 2231]

Uber's business model is designed to provide convenience and flexibility to consumers, allowing them to access various services through a single platform. The company's platform is available in over 70 countries around the world, with operations in more than 15,000 cities.

[10-K Line 2234]

Uber's technology platform is built on top of its massive network, which consists of hundreds of millions of drivers, consumers, merchants, shippers, and carriers. The company's leading technology includes marketplace, routing, and payments technologies that make it extremely efficient to launch new businesses and operationalize existing ones.

[10-K Line 2251]

Uber's business model is also designed to provide a seamless experience for its users, with features such as real-time tracking, estimated arrival times, and in-app messaging. The company's platform is available on both iOS and Android devices, making it accessible to a wide range of consumers.

[10-K Line 2233]

### Competitive Position and Moat
Uber faces significant competition in each of its three segments: Mobility, Delivery, and Freight. The company competes with other ridesharing companies such as Lyft, Bolt, Didi, and Ola, as well as traditional transportation services like taxis and public transportation.

[10-K Line 2265]

In the delivery segment, Uber competes with companies like DoorDash, Instacart, Gopuff, Rappi, Delivery Hero, Just Eat Takeaway, and Amazon. The company also faces competition from restaurants and other merchants that offer their own delivery services.

[10-K Line 2270]

Uber's competitive advantage lies in its massive network, leading technology, operational excellence, and product expertise. The company's platform is designed to provide a seamless experience for its users, with features such as real-time tracking, estimated arrival times, and in-app messaging.

[10-K Line 2251]

### Catalysts and Recent Developments
Uber has recently announced several catalysts that are expected to drive growth in the coming quarters. The company's Gross Bookings grew 20% year-over-year on a constant currency basis, driven by record audience and consumer engagement.

[EX-99.1 Line 8]

Uber's Non-GAAP Operating Income margins reached a record 3.9% of Gross Bookings, and Non-GAAP EPS grew 35%. The company's outlook for Q4 2026 includes Gross Bookings of $63.25 billion to $65.25 billion, representing growth of 18% to 22% year-over-year on a constant-currency basis.

[EX-99.1 Line 38]

Uber is also investing in autonomous vehicles, with approximately 700 fully driverless vehicles expected on Uber by year-end and over 2,000 in 2027.

[EX-99.1 Line 13]

### Insight:
Uber's platform has attracted 46 million Uber One members as of December 31, 2025 [10-K Line 2259].
