## 1. Overview

Apple designs, manufactures, and markets smartphones, personal computers, tablets, wearables, and accessories. It also sells related services. [10-K Line 936]

Its product groups include iPhone, Mac, iPad, and Wearables, Home and Accessories. [10-K Lines 938-1001] Its services include advertising, AppleCare, cloud services, and digital content. [10-K Lines 1003-1013]

The fiscal 2026 third quarter ended June 27, 2026. Apple reported quarterly revenue of $109.4 billion and diluted EPS of $2.02. [EX-99.1 Line 12]

## 2. Market Positioning and Growth Outlook

Apple operates an integrated product and services portfolio. Its six software platforms support experiences across Apple devices and services such as the App Store, Apple Music, Apple Pay, iCloud, and Apple TV. [EX-99.1 Line 19]

The company reported June-quarter records for total company revenue and EPS. iPhone, Mac, and Services revenue also set June-quarter records. [EX-99.1 Lines 8-9]

Recent catalysts include the all-new Siri AI and new software features introduced at WWDC26. [EX-99.1 Line 13] The installed base of active devices reached an all-time high across all major product categories and geographic segments. [EX-99.1 Line 14]

Risks include inflation, interest rates, and currency changes. [10-K Line 1354] Tariffs can affect the supply chain, component availability, pricing, and gross margin. The final effect remains uncertain. [10-K Line 1357]
