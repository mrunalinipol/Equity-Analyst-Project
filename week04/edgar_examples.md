# Week 4 EDGAR examples

Open both SEC filings and confirm the classification before Session 6.

## Spinoff: Vylor Inc. from Corteva, Inc.

- Parent: Corteva, Inc.
- New company: Vylor Inc. (`VYLR`)
- Record date: September 24, 2026.
- Distribution date: October 1, 2026.
- Ratio: one Vylor share for each Corteva share.
- Final information statement and completion notice: [Vylor 8-K, accession 0001193125-26-402928](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2128626/000119312526402928/ck0002128626-20260924.htm).
- Filing section to open: the information-statement cover for the parent, ratio, record date, and distribution date.
- Filing section to open: `Capitalization` and the unaudited pro forma notes. They state that Vylor expected to draw $1.5 billion on a 364-day revolver, $1.578 billion on a five-year revolver, and issue $1.1 billion of notes, net of an estimated $3.499 billion distribution to New Corteva and approximately $39 million of debt-issuance costs.
- Classification evidence: the filing states that Corteva distributes all issued Vylor shares pro rata to Corteva holders. It also states the one-for-one ratio and October 1, 2026 distribution date.

## Not a spinoff: Sagard Private Income Fund Inc.

- Filing: [Form 10-12G, accession 0001193125-26-436342](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2133018/000119312526436342/d488260d1012g.htm).
- Filed: October 28, 2026.
- Classification evidence: the `Explanatory Note` states that Sagard filed voluntarily so it could elect BDC status and provide public information.
- The filing does not describe a parent distributing this registrant's shares, and it contains no spinoff information statement.
- The shares are not exchange listed. The filing describes a non-exchange-traded, perpetual-life BDC.
