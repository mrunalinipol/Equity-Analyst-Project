# Week 4 reading notes — student-authored lines

These two answers must be written by the student after reading the assigned chapters.

## Greenblatt, Chapter 3

Who sells a spinoff in its first months, and why does that have nothing to do with its value?

Student line: The parent’s existing shareholders, especially index funds and institutions that cannot or do not want to hold the smaller spinoff, sell its shares; this forced selling reflects portfolio rules and investor preferences, not the spinoff’s underlying value.

## Klarman, Chapter 10

When is the parent the cheap piece?

Student line: The parent can become the cheap piece before the distribution when investors sell it to buy the spinoff they prefer, or after the distribution when investors treat the remaining parent as the unwanted leftover.
