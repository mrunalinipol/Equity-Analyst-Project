The value is in Exhibit 99.1 under **Costco Wholesale Corporation — Consolidated Statements of Income**. The table is labeled **dollars in millions, except per-share data (unaudited)**. Use the **16 Weeks Ended August 30, 2026** column and the **Total revenue** row.

The table shows:

Check: $93,873 million + $1,850 million = $95,723 million.

The $93.9 billion value in the opening paragraph is net sales. It is not total revenue.
