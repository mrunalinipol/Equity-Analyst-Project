## 1. Overview

### Business Model and Operations
Costco operates membership warehouses and e-commerce sites based on the concept of offering low prices on a limited selection of nationally-branded and private-label products in a wide range of categories, resulting in high sales volumes and rapid inventory turnover. This business model enables Costco to operate profitably at significantly lower gross margins than most other retailers [10-K Line 801]. The company buys most of its merchandise directly from suppliers and routes it to cross-docking consolidation points (depots) or directly to its warehouses, creating freight volume and handling efficiencies that lower costs associated with traditional multiple-step distribution channels [10-K Line 802].
